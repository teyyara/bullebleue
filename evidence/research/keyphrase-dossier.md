## Design and implementation implications

- French-first, welcoming, calm and age-inclusive design; avoid infantilizing or clinical presentation.
- Explain what the initiative is, who it welcomes, examples of activities, the first contact step and volunteer interest.
- Use the publicly published email as a mailto contact; do not guarantee delivery.
- Omit disputed address and stale opening times pending direct confirmation.
- No sensitive personal-data form or request for diagnoses/health details.
- No identifiable photos of participants or children without permission; initial illustrations are original CSS.
- Static dependency-free implementation. Canonical URL/sitemap require a confirmed domain.
