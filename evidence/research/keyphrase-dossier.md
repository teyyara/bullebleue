## Stronger historical context from APAiL reporting

**Info-Chalon, 13 October 2025**, reports APAiL's 30th anniversary celebration on 11 October 2025. It identifies Solange Chevat as the association's founder in December 1995 and describes values of solidarity, respect and sharing. It says APAiL's work reaches beyond Chalon, including Romania, Burkina Faso, Madagascar and Morocco, and describes educational projects and support for women's local initiatives including sewing, hairdressing, livestock and gardening. It says projects are discussed and followed with the people concerned.

The same article describes La Bulle Bleue as a local welcome/listening initiative outside school time, for children from age six, teenagers and adults who may feel uncomfortable in social settings because of invisible disability or other difficulties. At the 2025 anniversary event, young participants performed gentle percussion and older participants sang with support from a musician and an educator.

## Content implications

Recommended expanded storytelling:
- distinguish clearly between **La Bulle Bleue** (the local welcome initiative) and **APAiL** (the association carrying it);

## Design implications

Proposed motion:

- staggered but restrained section/card entrances;
- subtle image hover/focus treatments and gallery transitions;
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keyboard navigation and touch interaction;
- use native CSS and small amounts of vanilla JavaScript only; no paid animation framework.

## Rights, privacy and safeguarding

The intended audience includes minors and people who may be vulnerable. Never use identifiable participants or personal names from social posts merely because they are publicly visible. Require association approval and appropriate image permissions/releases for all identifiable people, with guardian consent for minors where required. Avoid health/diagnostic labels in image captions. Until permissions exist, use original AI-generated/illustrative imagery with no identifiable real person, clearly treated as illustrative rather than documentary.
