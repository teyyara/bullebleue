# CR-010 — Playful, bubbly and arts-led owner direction

**Received:** 5 November 2026  
**Status:** ADOPTED AND PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED IN LOOP 017  
**Authority:** Association-owner feedback relayed directly by the project user.

## Request

The owner dislikes rectangular UI treatments and asks for bubble-like shapes instead. She wants the entire website to become fun, childish, playful and artistic rather than overly professional, including more informal typography. At the homepage scroll ending, in the section headed **DERRIÈRE LA BULLE** with contact links, she wants the supplied assets/gathering.jpeg. She also wants the current placeholder homepage drawing replaced by a creative composition using all five supplied drawings in assets/dessin1.jpeg through assets/dessin5.jpeg.

## Decision and implementation

- Adopt a brighter, more playful visual direction while retaining the supplied blue logo as the identity anchor.
- Apply soft, irregular bubble geometry to cards, content panels, buttons, navigation actions, image frames and shared page surfaces.
- Use local system-font fallbacks with a friendly handwritten appearance; no remote font download or paid dependency is introduced.
- Replace the old hero-community.svg sticky background with a five-image drawing collage using the five supplied JPEGs. The composition is decorative and remains behind the three scroll-story chapters.
- Keep the existing two close-up activity photographs in the responsive homepage gallery.
- Record gathering.jpeg as the owner's selected closing-section image, but do not publish it yet: visual inspection shows recognizable people in a group photograph, and image-specific participant/guardian releases are not recorded in the repository. The closing-photo requirement remains blocked until the releases are evidenced.

## Acceptance / validation

- Static validation asserts all five drawing files appear in the sticky homepage collage, the old placeholder SVG is no longer used, and the recognizable group photograph is not published while release evidence is absent.
- Playwright verifies all five drawing images are present and that all nine routes continue to load without responsive overflow at the five maintained viewport widths.
- The six homepage review screenshots are saved under artifacts/loop017-browser/ and attached to the Loop 017 quality workflow.
- A CI pass does not replace manual visual review or owner acceptance.

## Open follow-up

Add gathering.jpeg beside the closing contact content only after the association documents appropriate releases for identifiable participants and guardians of identifiable minors. Then capture and review the new closing composition on desktop and mobile.
