# CR-016 — About page and sitewide detail polish

**Received:** 6 November 2026  
**Status:** Adopted and implemented — Loop 024 complete

## Request
Fix the remaining website imperfections, especially on Qui sommes-nous, and polish small visual details across the other public pages.

## Findings
- The seven activity labels under “Jouer, créer, découvrir” inherited a legacy parent divider and compressed list spacing. Generic card styles then made the rows look like crowded strips rather than intentional cards.
- The body background used small circles placed at fixed document percentages. On long pages, the circles drifted away from the page sections and appeared as detached dots beside unrelated content, notably beside the volunteers section on Qui sommes-nous.

## Implementation
- Rebuilt the seven activity labels as individual, comfortably spaced cards with readable padding and line wrapping.
- Used a balanced two-column activity grid on wider viewports and a single-column stack on mobile.
- Removed the legacy parent divider and its bottom-line treatment from the activity list.
- Removed the fixed document-level dot background while preserving intentional decorative details attached to the news cards, illustrations and sections.
- Added static source assertions and responsive Playwright checks for all seven labels, responsive columns, gutters, card borders, divider removal and page-level background treatment.
- Captured 30 screenshots across all nine public routes. Manually inspected six representative desktop/mobile views.

## Boundaries
No copy, factual information, photographs, drawings, testimonial attribution, legal details or association permissions were changed. No dependencies or deployment were added.
