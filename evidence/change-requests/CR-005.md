# CR-005 — Official logo, photography and logo-derived colour direction

**Received:** 2026-11-03  
**Status:** Partially implemented in Loop 010; palette calibration remains open.

## User direction

The user supplied a transparent association logo and original images in `assets/`, reports that the owner authorised the use of Facebook-page assets, and requested that:
- the provisional BB mark be replaced with the supplied logo;
- selected photos be placed in relevant sections of the site;
- the complete site's artistic direction and colours be derived from the official logo.

## Asset review

Eleven JPGs and one PNG were present in the repository. Four close-up photographs show activity details without identifiable faces and were selected for the homepage and activities page. Other photos show identifiable participants, including children, and remain unused pending confirmation of image-specific participant/guardian consent. User-reported owner permission is recorded; individual release status was not independently inspected.

## Implementation

- Official transparent logo is referenced locally in all six page headers and footers.
- One activity photograph appears in the homepage hero.
- Three activity photographs appear on `activites.html`.
- All placed photographs use descriptive alt text, lazy loading below the fold and local paths.
- Tests and asset register updated.

## Open palette item

The logo PNG blob is 1,062,882 bytes. The available GitHub file reader returned an empty content field for the binary file, preventing visual sampling and reliable hex-colour extraction. Existing CSS colour tokens were deliberately not misrepresented as logo-derived. Direct inspection of the PNG is still required to complete the requested colour-led art direction.
