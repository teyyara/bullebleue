# CR-011 — Correct homepage photo density, distribute supplied photos and refine visual art direction

**Received:** 5 November 2026  
**Status:** ADOPTED — LOOP 018  
**Owner feedback:** The eleven assets/posts photographs must not be shown as a large album on the homepage. Only the selected gathering.jpeg belongs beside the homepage's closing DERRIÈRE LA BULLE/contact content. The other photographs should be placed where they make contextual sense elsewhere in the website. The Actualités article cards still read as rectangles, and some text/background colour pairings are difficult to read.

## Requirements

1. Keep the homepage light: retain the five-drawing sticky story and gathering.jpeg at the closing contact area; remove all assets/posts imagery from index.html.
2. Display eight relevant activity photographs beside the activities content on Qui sommes-nous.
3. Display the three group/outing photographs in a separate, explicitly undated image strip on Actualités. State that these images are not attached to the three dated stories.
4. Redesign the three news article cards as strongly organic bubble/shape compositions, with curved image frames and responsive stacked mobile cards.
5. Make the shared palette deliberate and contrast-safe: deep navy/blue on pale surfaces, light text only on dark panels, remove low-contrast pale-blue emphasized text on pale backgrounds, and correct the light page-intro/dark text mismatch.
6. Correct the solidarity panel, whose dark gradient previously conflicted with its dark body text.
7. Add desktop/mobile visual captures for the actual news page and about page, and test photo distribution, bubble radii and computed text colours.

## Implementation

- Homepage: gathering.jpeg remains the single documentary photograph.
- Qui sommes-nous: eight activity photographs form a compact responsive image gallery.
- Actualités: three undated group/outing photographs sit separately from the dated article cards; dated card SVGs remain clearly labelled illustrations.
- CSS: asymmetrical bubble cards, pastel paper/mint/lemon/lilac surfaces, deep-blue text and restrained coral decoration. Text/background pairs have explicit dark/light colour roles.
- Validator and Playwright assertions protect the photo distribution, news-card bubble shape and readable text colours.

## Constraints

- The association owner supplied all assets and verbally authorized their visible use on the website; that authorization remains the project permission basis.
- Do not attach general activity/group photographs to specific dated news without evidence that they depict that specific event.
- Do not add paid dependencies or remote fonts.
- No production deployment.
