The 2025 event included a performance by young participants.

Complete provenance is in `evidence/research/keyphrase-dossier.md`.

## 3. Impact analysis

### Product/content
- Existing site under-represents the actual variety of the association's recent activity.
- The APAiL umbrella mission and La Bulle Bleue's local initiative should be described as two connected but distinct levels.
- Add a news/activity gallery or “La vie à la Bulle” section with examples, captions and dates only when supported.
- Preserve omission of exact venue and opening schedule until directly confirmed.
- Do not promise recurring events based on one-off outing posts.

### Design
- More real photography is requested.
- The 2024 Info-Chalon photo of the APAiL/Bulle Bleue stand reveals an existing blue bubble identity, posters, children's drawings and a collage/photo display. Use it as visual reference only until press/photo rights are cleared.
- A third-party mirror contains recent activity photos, but the images include identifiable people and may include minors. They must not be copied into the production site without appropriate written permission and image releases.
- Preferred safe interim approach: commission/obtain approved original association photos; use original AI-generated illustrations only as clearly illustrative assets where actual photos are unavailable.

### Motion / performance / accessibility
- Add subtle CSS bubble movement, image reveals, small entrance transitions and hover/focus details.
- Keep animations short, non-essential and unobtrusive.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not animate essential text in a way that delays reading, move focus, or make controls harder to use.
- Keep assets local, optimized and responsive (WebP/AVIF where supported, `srcset`, explicit dimensions, lazy loading below the fold).
- Continue the zero-paid-dependency policy; do not introduce remote animation frameworks, paid stock subscriptions or tracking.

### Security / privacy / safeguarding
- No registration or intake form is requested.
- Do not solicit diagnoses or sensitive personal information.
- Avoid naming participants from social posts.
- Require association approval and appropriate consent before publishing identifiable participants, especially minors.
- Captions must not infer diagnosis, impairment or treatment outcomes.

## 5. Proposed design direction

An authentic, warm editorial association website:
- use the actual blue bubble mark as a core visual motif once the official asset is obtained;
- richer blues, warm off-white and friendly accent colors, avoiding a generic clinical aesthetic;

## 6. Image rights, privacy and safeguarding

The FindGlocal mirror exposes recent photos associated with the Bulle Bleue Facebook page, and Info-Chalon published photographs of the association's stand and anniversary event. Public visibility is not publication permission. Do not copy, hotlink, crop, download into the website, or otherwise republish any such image until APAiL and the relevant photographer/rightsholder have authorized use.

For identifiable participants:
- obtain explicit publication permission/release from the participant where appropriate;
- obtain guardian consent for identifiable minors where required;
- confirm scope, channels and duration of permission;
- avoid names, personal stories, diagnoses or sensitive captions;
- remove or replace any asset if permission is withdrawn or cannot be evidenced.

Until this is documented, use original illustrative assets generated specifically for the site, neutral still-life/activity photographs that contain no identifiable people, or association-supplied images with recorded permission. AI-generated images must be clearly treated as illustrations and must not be presented as documentary evidence of an actual event or participant.

All images should be optimized, locally hosted when practical, given meaningful alt text (or empty alt for purely decorative images), rendered with intrinsic dimensions and lazy-loaded below the fold.
