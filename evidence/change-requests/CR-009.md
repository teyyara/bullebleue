## Research findings

- The JOAFE modification notice 20190009/1308, dated 27 February 2019, publishes the denomination: **A.P.AI.L. : AMITIÉ PARTAGE AIDE LOGISTIQUE, MATÉRIELLE ET MORALE** and lists 14, quai Gambetta, 71100 Chalon-sur-Saône.
- The City of Chalon-sur-Saône association directory currently lists “APAIL Aide Logistique Matérielle et Morale” at “QUAI GAMBETTA 14 CHALON SUR SAONE”.
- A public RNA/SIRENE-derived listing, showing an INSEE data update of 24 June 2026, associates APAiL with RNA **W712002504**, SIREN **519 970 743** and an address at **14 boulevard de la République**.
- The public records therefore conflict on address. The project cannot safely choose one and label it the current registered office without APAiL confirmation.

## Implementation

- The legal page now shows the JOAFE-published denomination and the registry-listed RNA/SIREN identifiers, with a confirmation caveat.
- The address remains pending. The record conflict is described transparently; neither location is asserted as the current registered office.
- Publication manager and host remain pending.
- Added public source links, internal source provenance, an assumption, a decision record and requirement REQ-048.
- Extended the static validator to protect the legal identity details and address caveat.
