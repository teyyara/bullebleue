# CR-008 — Trustworthy visual evidence for the sticky homepage and activity gallery

**Received:** 2026-11-03  
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED IN LOOP 015 — CI EXECUTION AND VISUAL REVIEW PENDING.

## Finding from the Loop 014 screenshot review

The Loop 014 artifact contained three files. Direct inspection identified:
- The desktop/mobile full-page captures showed blank gallery placeholders because the second screenshot navigation did not force the lazy-loaded images to load before capture.
- The final story screenshot captured the active panel before its 520 ms opacity transition completed, leaving the text nearly transparent.
- Full-page captures of a sticky, viewport-sized illustration are misleading: a tall full-page screenshot is not a simulation of scrolling through the sticky story.

The existing page-level Playwright tests did verify that images load when eagerly requested and that all three story chapters become current. The flaw was in the separate screenshot-capture sequence and its timing.

## Implementation

- Keep the website's lazy-loaded images and existing story animation unchanged.
- Add screenshot helpers that explicitly eager-load and decode all local images before gallery captures.
- Wait for the requested story chapter to be current and its computed panel opacity to reach at least 0.99 before taking a screenshot.
- Capture each of the three desktop story states in a normal viewport, not as a tall full-page screenshot.
- Capture the gallery as a dedicated section screenshot on desktop and mobile after its two photographs load and decode.
- Rename the screenshot directory/artifact to Loop 015 and update the README, requirements, product contract and decision log.

## Validation

CI must pass Python structural validation, Node syntax/interaction regression and all 45 existing page/viewport browser checks. The uploaded six-image artifact must be inspected directly; no manual visual PASS is claimed until the new screenshots are opened and reviewed.
