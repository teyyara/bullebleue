# CR-006 — Owner-led homepage, navigation and editorial news structure

**Received:** 2026-11-03  
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED IN LOOP 012; source audit and runtime checks to be recorded in the loop checkpoint.

## Owner feedback

The association owner requested:

1. A homepage with drawings used as a background illustration. As the visitor scrolls, the background remains in place while the text changes/appears progressively.
2. A dedicated **Qui sommes-nous ?** page.
3. A high-priority **Actualités** page showing only two or three rectangular cards, each with a small caption, image, short text and **Voir plus** action. Each card must open a dedicated article page with an image and article content.
4. A dedicated **Adhérer** page for people who wish to join the association.
5. A dedicated **Nous contacter** page.
6. A **Mentions légales** page.

## Loop 012 interpretation and implementation

- Replaced the separate homepage photo hero and destination-card-heavy top section with one local illustrated community scene, recoloured using the official logo-derived blue family.
- The illustration is sticky while three concise text chapters take turns as the visitor scrolls. IntersectionObserver is progressive enhancement only; all text is visible by default and reduced-motion users receive the non-animated version.
- Consolidated previous `La Bulle`, `Activités` and `L’APAiL` pages into `qui-sommes-nous.html`, preserving source-grounded audience, activity, volunteer and historical solidarity information.
- Primary navigation is now: Accueil, Qui sommes-nous ?, Actualités, Adhérer, Nous contacter. Mentions légales is available from every page footer.
- Actualités shows exactly three selected items from the supplied extraction: 26 October 2026, 17 October 2026 and 10 October 2026. Each opens its own root-level `actualite-*.html` article page.
- Article cards and articles use local original SVG illustrations. The images are explicitly labelled as illustrations and not as photographs of the specific dated events.
- Adhérer provides an email request for current terms. No membership fee, form, required document or renewal process is invented.
- Mentions légales is a clearly marked draft. Exact legal designation, registered office, publication manager and hosting provider remain unprovided and must be completed by APAiL before publication.
- Removed obsolete `la-bulle.html`, `activites.html` and `apail.html` entry points rather than leaving outdated, duplicate pages.
