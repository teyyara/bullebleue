**Received:** 6 November 2026  
**Status:** Adopted and implemented in Loop 023

## Request
Polish the small design details across the website, especially spacing, any leftover numbering and centered/aligned elements, without changing its overall visual identity or owner-approved content.

- Aligned the Contact page primary and location panels as equal-height flex columns, anchoring their lower information callouts consistently; prevented the primary email action from stretching across its card.

- Added structural and browser assertions for the removed numbering, retained historical dates, centered columns, and Contact card baseline alignment.
- Expanded the screenshot artifact to include all three article pages plus Adhérer, Contact and Mentions légales at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Boundaries
No owner-provided facts, images, dates or testimonials changed. The homepage narrative, original drawings, photo distribution and organic Actualités shapes are preserved. No paid dependency or deployment added.
