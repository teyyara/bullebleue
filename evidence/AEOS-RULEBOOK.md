- No evidence, no PASS. Implementation, build, runtime observation, quality, production readiness and business outcomes are separate claims.

- Every meaningful completed loop must leave a recoverable Git checkpoint and updated \`AEOS_STATUS.md\`.
- One standalone \`CONTINUE\` authorizes exactly one additional loop. No automatic continuation.
- Do not deploy or claim production validation without explicit deployment configuration/authorization and exact artifact evidence.
- Never store secrets in Git or evidence.
